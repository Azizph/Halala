    On questions nobody saw

    One logged test on 55 sealed questions. A right answer in Arabizi went from 317 tokens to 64, against 50 in English, and right answers from 2 to 8 of 11. The saving is on Arabizi: the other forms cost about the same. On this test, one-fifth Arabizi, tokens per right answer fell 17.5%, and less Arabizi means less saving. The gap is smaller, not closed.

    What didn't work

    • A word list for Saudi dialect: 45 to 48 of 100, short of the mark set beforehand, so it was dropped. Saudi is still open.
    • A learned router with repeated sampling: no better than the simple design, and slower.
    • Asking a model to rewrite Arabizi: every version lost to the free rules.
    • The first Arabizi rewrite: 2 of 15 on its first unseen test, so it was rebuilt around real Arabic words.
